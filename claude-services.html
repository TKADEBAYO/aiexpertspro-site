FAQ

Common questions, direct answers.

Why Claude specifically and not OpenAI / Gemini / open-source models?
Claude is what I deploy and what I'm partnered with. It's particularly strong at long-context reasoning, agentic workflows, and tool use — which is what most production deployments actually need. If your use case genuinely fits a different model better, I'll tell you in the Strategy Session. I'd rather lose a £750 booking than ship the wrong tool.
What's the difference between this and your governance practice?
Two distinct services for different audiences. The governance practice is for CROs at UK regulated firms who need an audit-ready position on the AI they already run — £3,500 fixed-fee diagnostic. Claude Partner Services are for any organisation that wants to deploy Claude into specific workflows. Different price, different scope, different buyer. Some firms book both.
Do you sign NDAs?
Yes, mutual NDA standard for any Sprint or extended engagement. Strategy Sessions are covered by an NDA on request. I work in regulated environments so confidentiality discipline is built in, not optional.
Can you build something that handles personal or sensitive data?
Yes — and this is where 20 years of regulated-sector experience matters. Sprint deliverables include data flow mapping, retention controls, and PII handling decisions documented from day one. If your use case touches GDPR, FCA Consumer Duty, healthcare data, or similar, I treat that as a feature of the engagement, not a complication.
What if our team has zero AI experience?
That's a Workshop scenario, not a Sprint. The Workshop teaches the team to use Claude properly before any production deployment. Skipping the adoption step means you ship a system nobody uses — which is what most AI projects fail at.
Can the Sprint be larger than 5 days?
Yes, but only after a Sprint or Strategy Session has run. I quote multi-week engagements only when there's a defensible scope. The fixed-fee 5-day model exists specifically to prevent the open-ended "consulting engagement that grows" pattern.
Who actually does the work?
I do. Folu Adebayo, principal at AIExpertsPro. Sprints and Workshops are not subcontracted to junior consultants. If a specialist contractor is needed for a specific build component (e.g. a niche integration), I tell you upfront and you approve their involvement.
